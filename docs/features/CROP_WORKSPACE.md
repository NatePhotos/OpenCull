# Crop workspace

Scroll the mouse wheel or trackpad over the crop image to tighten or loosen the crop around its centre. The aspect ratio and angle stay fixed, the crop stays inside the image, and a continuous scroll gesture is one crop-undo step. Scrolling over crop controls leaves those controls alone. Apply saves the crop through the existing sidecar path; Cancel discards it.

Settings → Zoom → Hide filmstrip while cropping is experimental and off by default. It temporarily hides both the scene's frame strip and the bottom navigation strip. Applying or cancelling restores the previous visibility. The saved Filmstrip preference is never changed. The option is included in settings import/export.
